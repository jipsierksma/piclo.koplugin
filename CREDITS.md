## Puzzle art

Every pack lists its own license and credit in its data file. `make validate`
checks that each shipped pack appears in the table below.

| Pack | Title | Puzzles | Provenance | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `starter` | First Steps | 4, 6×6 to 8×8 | Original pixel art made for this plugin | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), credit "Jip Sierksma" |
| `house` | Around the House | 11 × 10×10 | Original pixel art made for this plugin | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), credit "Jip Sierksma" |
| `woods` | In the Woods | 8 × 15×15 | Original pixel art made for this plugin | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), credit "Jip Sierksma" |

Every puzzle includes a shaded reveal layer, also original. None of the pictures
are copied or traced from other puzzle games or apps. The two practice puzzles
in How to play (`piclo/data/practice.lua`) are under the same license.

as you give credit, link to the license, and say whether you changed them.
