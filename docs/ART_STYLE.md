# Art style

Each puzzle is one `art` grid of tone digits; `.` is empty and any digit is a filled cell in play.

- `0`: outlines and black accents (eyes, noses, stems, rims)
- `1`: dark materials
- `2`: mid tones
- `3`: light surfaces
- `4`: highlights only

Composition: a bold silhouette that reads in black alone (no outline-only drawings, no dotted textures); the subject fills the frame; light comes from the top left.
